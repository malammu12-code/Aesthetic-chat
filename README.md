# PIXEL PLAYER

An 8-bit music player that syncs with your Spotify. Here's how to get started.

### 1\. CONNECT SPOTIFY

Click "Connect Spotify" inside the app. You'll be sent to Spotify's official login page — sign in with your own Spotify account (Free or Premium).

### 2\. APPROVE ACCESS

Spotify asks you to approve the app. We only read your library and control playback — nothing is stored or shared. Approve and you're sent right back.

### 3\. START LISTENING

Search any track, build a queue, make playlists, and play. Premium accounts stream full songs; Free accounts get 30-second previews.

Everyone uses their own Spotify account — there's no shared login. Each listener connects once and they're set.
